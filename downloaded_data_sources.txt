DOWNLOADED AND EXTRACTED DATA FILES
====================================

1. brent_oil_price.csv
   Description: Monthly global Brent crude oil prices in US dollars per barrel.
   Source: FRED / Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis
   Direct download link: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/graph/fredgraph.csv?id=POILBREUSDM
   Source series page: https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/POILBREUSDM

2. cbn_mpr_decisions_2017_2025.csv
   Description: Extracted CBN Monetary Policy Committee decision dates and MPR values from 2017 to 2025.
   Source: Central Bank of Nigeria, MPC Communiques / Key Decisions
   Direct link: https://www.cbn.gov.ng/monetarypolicy/decisions.html

3. cbn_mpr_monthly_2010_2025.csv
   Description: Monthly MPR series compiled by carrying each effective CBN decision forward to the end of each month.
   Underlying source link: https://www.cbn.gov.ng/monetarypolicy/decisions.html
   Coverage note: Values from 2017 onward are populated from the CBN decisions page. Months before 2017-09 remain blank because historical decisions are not reliably exposed by that page extraction.

4. CBN_MPR_2010_2025.xlsx
   Description: Excel workbook containing the extracted MPC decisions, compiled monthly MPR series, and coverage notes.
   Underlying source link: https://www.cbn.gov.ng/monetarypolicy/decisions.html

5. nigeria_inflation_model_data.csv
   Description: Single compiled monthly model-data CSV used by main.py.
   Files used: brent_oil_price.csv, cbn_mpr_monthly_2010_2025.csv, and World Bank annual CPI/exchange-rate data.
   Important status: NBS monthly Inflation/Food_Index values are blank. MPR is blank before the CBN page's archived coverage begins. The World Bank CPI and exchange rate are annual interpolations for continuity and are not substitutes for monthly NBS/CBN observations.
